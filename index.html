How to play

Guess today’s five-letter first name in 6 tries.

Start with any real five-letter first name. No letters are given before your first guess.

After each guess, green means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means the right letter in a different spot, and gray means the letter is not in the name.

There’s one new name each day at your local midnight. Miss a day? Just play the new one. Share your grid without spoiling the answer.

Example: if the answer were JAMES, this guess shows E in the wrong spot.
