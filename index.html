How to play

Guess today’s familiar first name in 6 tries. The daily changes between 4 and 7 letters—check the badge above the board.

Start with any real first name that fits the board. Everyone gets the same name for the same date. No letters are given before your first guess.

After each guess, bright green means the right letter in the right spot, yellow means the right letter in a different spot, and gray means the letter is not in the name.

Want a second challenge? Tap Try obscure for a harder five-letter puzzle. Both change at your local midnight, but only the official daily counts toward your streak and friends board. Share either grid without spoiling the answer.
