How to play

Guess today’s five-letter first name in 6 tries.

J is given. E is in the name. The answer could be JAMES.

The first letter is already on the board. Type the rest of the name, or the whole name. Each guess must be a real first name with both clue letters.

There’s one new name each day at your local midnight. Miss a day? Just play the new one. Share your grid without spoiling the answer.
