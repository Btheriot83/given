Support

Need a hand? Email support@namedgame.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within a day.

How to play

Guess the day’s first name in six tries. The official daily is 4 to 7 letters. Green means the right letter in the right spot, gold (yellow on the web) means the right letter in a different spot, and dark means the letter isn’t in the name. Every guess must be a real first name of the right length.

When does the new name arrive?

At midnight in your time zone. Only the official daily counts toward your streak and the friends board.

Named+ (iPhone)

Named+ is a one-time purchase that adds the Obscure daily, unlimited practice at 5, 6 or 7 letters, every past daily in the archive, and themed name packs. The official daily is always free.

  • Restore a purchase: open Settings (the gear) and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple ID you bought with.
  • Redeem an offer code: Settings → Redeem code.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Friends board

Tap the trophy, pick a nickname, then create a code or enter a friend’s. Share the invite link; your score posts automatically when you finish the official daily. To leave, choose Use another code. To have an entry removed early, email us with the board code and nickname.

Daily reminder

Settings → Daily reminder. If it doesn’t arrive, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Named.

Privacy

No account, and your stats stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
